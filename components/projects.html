School IERP Portal

An Integrated Educational Resource Planning (IERP) system designed for Janahit Secondary School in Jomsom, Nepal. Built with React and structured around high-fidelity Figma designs, the system streamlines administration, timetable coordination, grade publication, and real-time attendance management.

Web Application School ERP UI Design Responsive Design

01. The Problem

Janahit Secondary School in Jomsom, Nepal faced extreme operational overhead with manual attendance tracking, offline exam result publications, paper-based leave requests, and manual timetabling. This caused data silos and administrative delays.

02. User Research

Conducted field research and interviewed 10 teachers, 25 students, and the principal. Key insights: Teachers spent 15% of class time doing paperwork, and parents lacked access to real-time attendance and academic performance updates.

03. User Persona

Bijay, 38 (Math Teacher): "I want a secure, visual way to log daily attendance and post homework announcements without carrying heavy paper ledgers. It must load quickly on mobile devices in low-connectivity zones."

04. Wireframing & Design System

Mapped out dedicated dashboards for Admins, Teachers, and Students. Designed a responsive grid system with clean typography, prominent status indicators, and clear color coding for visual scanning.

05. High-Fidelity UI Design

Created clean interfaces with high visual contrast. Key metrics (like attendance percentages and term averages) are rendered as rounded gauges. Timetables are laid out as modular cards with active status colors.

06. Outcomes & Metrics

-75% reduction in weekly attendance logging time. Over 90% parent satisfaction within 2 months of launching, due to the instant announcements notice board and online grade card publication.

07. Lessons Learned

Offline robustness is key. Jomsom's alpine terrain leads to intermittent network failures. Building localized storage models for core data and utilizing aggressive visual caching ensures the portal stays usable offline.

APX Fitness Tracker

An all-in-one health and physical activity tracking application designed to simplify logging workouts, checking BMI ratings, scheduling sleep/hydration, and managing daily alerts. APX brings together multiple scattered metrics into a unified mobile dashboard.

Mobile App Health & Fitness UI Design Wireframing

01. The Problem

Most fitness applications focus strictly on either exercise logging or nutrition, forcing users to switch between multiple apps to track sleep, hydration, BMI, and daily workouts. This fragments their health tracking experience.

02. User Research

Surveyed 80 fitness enthusiasts and gym beginners. Over 70% stated they struggled to remain consistent because tracking required too much manual input across multiple platforms, leading to user fatigue.

03. User Persona

Rohan, 27 (Software Engineer): "I want an all-in-one app that tracks my gym routines, reminds me to drink water, and helps me keep an eye on my BMI without navigating complex charts."

04. Wireframing & Design System

Planned a clean, modular tab system containing: Dashboard, Workouts (Aerobics, Strength, Stretching), and Tools (BMI Calculator, Hydration Tracker). Focus on simple visual rings and quick-tap logging.

05. High-Fidelity UI Design

Implemented a dark mobile theme using high-contrast active gradients. Used visually striking dashboard components with clear data representations for different exercise types.

06. Outcomes & Metrics

Average onboarding time decreased, and logging friction was minimized. Beta users reported a 60% increase in daily hydration consistency and workout tracking regularity.

07. Lessons Learned

Tracking physical activity requires micro-incentives. Integrating smart reminders and simple motivational quotes kept engagement high without annoying the user.

Food Scheduler

A streamlined application empowering users to schedule and manage their morning, afternoon, and evening meals effectively. Designed to simplify nutrition tracking and ensure a balanced daily diet without the clutter.

Mobile App Lifestyle & Health UI Design Prototyping

01. The Problem

People struggle to maintain a consistent eating schedule amidst busy lifestyles, often skipping meals or relying on unstructured diet tracking that leads to poor nutritional habits.

02. User Research

Interviews with working professionals revealed that they prefer visual planning over rigid calorie counting. A simple morning, afternoon, and evening scheduling approach was highly requested.

03. User Persona

Maya, 29 (Marketing Manager): "I don't have time to weigh my food. I just want to plan my breakfast, lunch, and dinner ahead of time so I stop ordering fast food at the last minute."

04. Wireframing & Design System

Focused on a clean, appetizing color palette utilizing soft greens and warm tones. The navigation is structured around time blocks (Morning, Afternoon, Evening) for intuitive daily scheduling.

05. High-Fidelity UI Design

Developed rich visual cards for meals, incorporating mouth-watering imagery and clear typography to make the planning process feel rewarding rather than like a chore.

06. Outcomes & Metrics

The simplified 3-block daily scheduling approach increased user retention by 45% compared to granular meal-tracking competitors during beta testing.

07. Lessons Learned

Visual appeal is critical in food-related applications. High-quality imagery combined with a minimalist interface directs focus to the meals, increasing user engagement and adherence.
